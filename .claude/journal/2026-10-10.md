## Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture | One Go service, API plus a sync worker goroutine | Only the Google call needs isolating. Microservices add deploys and a broker for no gain at this size. |
| Signature | HMAC-SHA256 over `timestamp.body`, 5-minute window | The brief says "secret signature". Ed25519 was considered; it fits many partners better, not one. |
| Duplicates | Match by external ID, then email; ID and email on different leads is 409 | Satisfies R1.3 and R1.4 without silently merging two leads. |
| Sync tracking | `leads.version` vs `lead_sync.synced_version`, one row per lead | Coalesces repeated updates and re-converges after a crash. |
| Packages | `internal/api` holds all handlers instead of `internal/webhook` | Health and OAuth routes are not webhooks. |

## Lessons

- `kill %1` does nothing in a non-interactive shell. A stale server held :8080 and caused false 404s. Kill by PID.
- `pkill -f` with a pattern that also appears in the shell's own command line kills the shell.
